WHAT UNIFUSION DOES
UNIFUSION is offline software on one Android phone. It takes in files and notes as SOURCE records and finds how they relate (KIN). A local AI model may only propose summaries and links. Nothing becomes an official record until a person approves it (HUMAN_FINAL). Approved records go into one registry (REG) with SHA-256 fingerprints and an append-only log, so a later check can show if something was changed or swapped. A visual view (Living Universe) shows the records and their links. Today it is a working prototype (TRL 4) on one phone with 104 approved records, no outside users yet, and three Canadian patent applications filed (none granted).
